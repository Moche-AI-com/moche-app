Questions

The things hosts ask before they build a Dome.

Do I have to connect my Airbnb account?

No. Moche.AI is completely platform-agnostic. Guests reach the portal by QR code or link during the stay — no Airbnb login, no Vrbo connection, no PMS integration. Your property data belongs to you, not to a platform's API.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

The Dome uses retrieval-augmented generation — it answers only from your uploaded documents, and can cite the source. When confidence is low, it escalates to you rather than inventing anything. Its instruction is simple: inform, never invent.

How long does setup take?

Most hosts finish conversational onboarding in 15–20 minutes, and the portal can be live before the next check-in. A Brain Health score tracks completeness and the AI proactively suggests gaps to fill based on common guest questions.

Can I run it alongside Hospitable or HostBuddy?

Yes — they're complementary. Keep your pre-arrival messaging tool for booking confirmations and check-in reminders. Moche is the in-stay layer for the WiFi, appliance and local questions that come up once guests are physically inside your property.

What happens if I cancel?

You own your data. Export your Property Brain documents and structured profile at any time. Because the Brain is built on your unique property details, the value compounds the longer you host — but you're never locked in.
